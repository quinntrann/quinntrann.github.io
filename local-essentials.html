Local Essentials

A new place feels more familiar when the everyday basics are in reach. Start with groceries and a pharmacy, then prepare your phone and banking questions.

On this page

  • Your first shopping trip
  • Phone service
  • Banking questions
  • Student support

Your first shopping trip

Choose a store near your home or campus. Check its hours and your route before leaving, especially if you will carry shopping bags on the way back.

Groceries and household basics

Make a short list of food, toiletries, and cleaning supplies. Use a store's own directory to confirm its address and available services.

  • Find Safeway stores in Tempe

Find a pharmacy

A store's opening hours and its pharmacy hours may differ. Confirm the pharmacy's hours directly, and ask its staff about prescription services before your visit.

  • Search Walgreens stores and pharmacies

Before you leave

  • Save the store's address and opening hours.
  • Bring your shopping list and a reusable bag.
  • Plan both your outward and return trips.

Phone service

Before choosing a plan, ask the provider to check your specific phone and explain the full cost. Keep these questions handy when comparing options:

  • Will my current phone work on your network, and does it need to be unlocked?
  • Can I use a physical SIM or an eSIM with this phone?
  • What is the total monthly cost, including taxes and any activation fees?
  • How much data is included, and how do renewal and cancellation work?

Banking questions

Gather information before opening an account. Ask the bank or credit union which identification and address documents it accepts, and request its fee schedule.

  • Is there a monthly fee or a minimum balance requirement?
  • Which cash machines can I use without an additional fee?
  • What happens if a payment exceeds my available balance?
  • What fees apply to sending or receiving international transfers?
  • Read the CFPB bank account guides and opening checklist

Student support

ASU students can start with the International Students and Scholars Center for university resources. For health service locations, appointments, and coverage questions, use ASU Health Services.

  • Browse ASU's local resources

  • Find ASU Health Services
