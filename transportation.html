Compare your options

Start with where you need to go. A trip within your neighborhood may need a different option from a journey between campuses.

Local and campus travel at a glance
Travel option What to plan for
Bus and light rail For trips across the Phoenix area. Check your stops, direction, transfers, and current fare before leaving.
Tempe streetcar Another option near the Tempe campus. Check whether its route serves both your starting point and destination.
ASU intercampus shuttle For ASU students, faculty, and staff traveling between campuses. Check the route and service calendar.
Walking or biking Consider the distance and weather for shorter trips. Check campus paths, bike parking, and walk-only zones.

Plan your first trip

  1. Save your destination. Use the full address or campus building name, rather than only the neighborhood.
  2. Plan the journey out and back. Enter your travel date and time in the trip planner. Check the boarding stop, direction, transfers, and the return service you will need.
  3. Check before leaving. Review current fares, payment instructions, and service alerts on Valley Metro's website. Allow extra time to find an unfamiliar stop.
  • Plan a trip with Valley Metro
  • Check Valley Metro fares and service updates

Keep a copy of your route. Save the stop names and return directions on your phone so you can refer to them along the way.

Travel around ASU

ASU's intercampus shuttles connect the Downtown Phoenix, Polytechnic, Tempe, and West Valley campuses for students, faculty, and staff. Check the shuttle page for routes, the service calendar, and live tracking.

If you plan to bike, ASU's bike guide links to campus maps and explains registration, parking, and walk-only zones. Review the route before your first visit, especially when traveling to a new building.

  • Review ASU bike and scooter resources
  • Find ASU parking and transportation support
